Dataset terms of use

ConsentVid datasets have no blanket licence. Each clip may be used only within the terms of its own consent receipt: a record in the contributor's ATproto repository, signed by their key, referenced from the manifest as cv:consentReceipt.

What a receipt grants

  • purposes: e.g. training, evaluation, benchmarking, researchOnly.
  • modelClasses: who may use it, e.g. academic, openWeights, commercial, foundationModel.
  • modalities: which signals, e.g. video, audio, transcript, face, voice.
  • expiresAt: optional end date of the permission.

A use is permitted only if the receipt lists every applicable purpose, model class and modality. Anything not listed is not granted.

Withdrawal

Contributors can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the receipt (consent to processing personal data must be withdrawable, GDPR Art. 7(3)). Before every use, and at least before each training run, run:

consentvid resync manifest.json --write manifest.clean.json

and use only the clips that remain. resync verifies each receipt against the contributor's signed repository; withdrawn, expired or modified clips must be deleted from your copies.

Attribution and authorship

Cite the dataset as given in the manifest's citeAs. Contributors attest, under their signing key, that they recorded each clip or otherwise hold the rights to grant consent. If you believe a clip was contributed without that right, report it to the contributor's PDS operator and stop using it.
