Version 1.0, effective 21 November 2026. Issued by Evangelos Kazakos, maintainer of consentvid.

consentvid datasets have no blanket licence. Each clip may be used only within the terms of its own consent record: a record in the contributor’s own ATproto repository, signed with that repository’s key (in manifests, the field cv:consentReceipt). Anyone may build a dataset from public consent records; each clip may be used only within its own record’s terms.

What a consent record grants

  • purposes: what it may be used for, e.g. training, evaluation, benchmarking. (researchOnly is deprecated; older consent records that use it keep their literal meaning.)
  • modelClasses: who may use it, e.g. academic, open-weights, commercial. (foundationModel is deprecated; older consent records that use it keep their literal meaning.)
  • modalities: which signals, e.g. video, audio, transcript, face, voice.
  • expiresAt: optional end date of the permission.

A use is permitted only if the consent record lists every applicable purpose, model class and modality. Anything not listed is not granted.

compensation records what the contributor said they expect. It is a declaration, not a payment arrangement: consentvid neither brokers nor enforces payment.

People in the video and age

Every consent record states who identifiably appears (peoplePresent: only the contributor, everyone shown having agreed, or no identifiable people) and that the contributor is an adult able to consent (adultAttested). These are self-declared under the contributor’s key. A consent record without them is invalid. If you find a clip that contradicts its declaration, for example identifiable bystanders in a clip marked only-me, stop using it and report it. You can also email evangelos.kazakos@cvut.cz; until a labeler service exists, such a report cannot remove a record from the contributor’s account.

The command line, manifests and version-1 records spell these values in camelCase (openWeights, onlyMe, allConsented, noIdentifiablePeople); version-2 records use the kebab-case form. Both mean the same.

Bots, AI-usage preferences and labels

A consent record from an account that labels itself a bot (the bot self-label on its profile) is not valid consent. Nor is a consent record granting training from an account whose own published AI-usage preference (community.lexicon.preference.ai) denies training: the contributor’s preference wins. Clips the contributor labelled porn, sexual, nudity or graphic-media are left out of datasets unless you explicitly include them. resync checks all of this and drops such clips.

Withdrawal

Contributors can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the consent record (consent to processing personal data must be withdrawable, GDPR Art. 7(3)). Before every use, and at least before each training run, run:

consentvid resync manifest.json --write manifest.clean.json

and use only the clips that remain. resync verifies each consent record against the contributor’s signed repository; withdrawn, expired or modified clips must be deleted from your copies. Clips that resync could not check are dropped too.

For contributors: records are public

Your video and consent records are public ATproto records. Relays, Jetstream and anyone indexing the network copy them as you write them. Deleting a record withdraws consent, and well-behaved indexes drop it, but deletion is not erasure: copies made before it can persist. The records contain what you enter or choose, plus what the tools measure from the file: title, description, tags, languages; content hash, size, duration, width and height, file type; content labels; a pointer to the video (your post, your server, or another store); your consent terms and attestations; the time of signing; and your account identifier (DID). File names are never written to records (a URL you supply yourself is published as written). Publish nothing there you would not want copied. Deleting or deactivating your ATproto account does not delete the video bytes on R2, IPFS or PeerTube: run consentvid revoke <video-uri> --delete-bytes for each video first. Files on your own server and Bluesky posts are deleted with your account. IPFS copies pinned by others cannot be recalled.

Models trained before a withdrawal

GDPR Art. 7(3): “The withdrawal of consent shall not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.” Under these terms, a model trained in good faith on clips that were valid at a resync made before training stays legitimate and need not be destroyed. From the moment of withdrawal, the clip may not be used again: not for new training, retraining, fine-tuning, continued training from a checkpoint, or new evaluations. Delete it from your copies.

Attribution and authorship

Cite the dataset as given in the manifest’s citeAs. Contributors attest, under their signing key, that they recorded each clip or otherwise hold the rights to grant consent. If you believe a clip was contributed without that right, stop using it and report the account to its hosting server or, on Bluesky, with Report account; if the account is taken down (by the host or at the relay), resync reports its clips as unavailable and drops them. You can also email evangelos.kazakos@cvut.cz; until a labeler service exists, such a report cannot remove a record from the contributor’s account.
