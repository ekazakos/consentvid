Contribute your Bluesky videos

Pick videos you posted on Bluesky, or upload one from your computer without posting it, and sign an explicit consent saying how they may be used for AI. Your videos stay on your own Bluesky server: withdraw here, or delete the post, and the video leaves every dataset.

Sign in with Bluesky

You'll be sent to your own server to approve. ConsentVid only asks to create and delete org.consentvid.* records in your account and to upload video files to it. It can't post, follow or read your private data.
