Give consent for a video

Choose videos you posted on Bluesky, or upload a file without posting it, and sign a consent record, kept in your own account, stating how they may be used. Withdraw here at any time; for a Bluesky post, deleting the post also works.

Withdrawing stops new use. It does not reach models already trained, and existing copies are not deleted automatically: the terms require dataset users to delete withdrawn clips when they next run resync.

Sign in with Bluesky

@

You’ll be sent to your own Bluesky server to approve. This page asks only for permission to add and remove its own consent and video records in your account, and to upload video files to it.

The exact permission 
atproto repo:org.consentvid.videoV2?action=create&action=delete repo:org.consentvid.consentV2?action=create&action=delete blob:video/*

If your server cannot grant these, the page asks you first before requesting the broader atproto transition:generic, which covers more than this page needs, including posting, following and deleting. This page writes only org.consentvid.* records (app/src/scopes.js).

What signing writes to your account

org.consentvid.videoV2title, description, tags, languages; content hash, size, duration, width and height, file type; content labels; a pointer to the video
org.consentvid.consentV2your consent terms and attestations; the time of signing

Both are public and carry your account’s identifier. File names are never written.
